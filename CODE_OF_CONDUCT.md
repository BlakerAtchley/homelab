# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This repository documents a home lab built to learn networking, cloud and security. Everyone who takes part, whether by opening an issue, suggesting a change or commenting, is expected to help keep this a respectful, welcoming and harassment-free place to learn, regardless of background, experience level or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give feedback that is specific and constructive.
- Accept corrections gracefully. Everyone here is learning.
- Keep discussion focused on the content of the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- Posting anyone's private information (addresses, phone numbers, credentials, account details) without their explicit permission.
- Sharing malicious links, malware or exploit code aimed at real systems.
- Spam, trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code of conduct, and may block accounts that repeatedly do so.

To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue, or the [GitHub abuse reporting form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse). Reports are reviewed promptly and handled as privately as possible.
